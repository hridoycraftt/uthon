Four
steps to
launch

Same rhythm on every project, so you always know what happens next and who is doing it.

01
Week 1

Clarify the brief

A structured questionnaire and one workshop to agree on goals, audience and limits.

02
Week 2–3

Concept & direction

Two or three routes, shown as short moodfilms, then one chosen together.

03
Week 4–8

Design & system

The route becomes a system: type, colour, motion, components and every key page.

04
Week 9–12

Build & launch

Built in Webflow, tested on real devices, handed over with a training call.
